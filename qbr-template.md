# QBR Template

A quarterly business review structure. Aim for 45 minutes with the customer, and one page of notes afterwards.

## Before the meeting

- Pull usage numbers for the quarter (spend, adoption, active users/campaigns).
- Read the last 90 days of tickets — note themes, not just count.
- Draft the outline with the QBR prep prompt in `ai-prompts-for-cs.md`.
- Send the agenda to the customer 3 days before.

## Agenda (45 min)

| Time | Section | Purpose |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–5 | Welcome + goal of the session | Set the tone: this is about their outcomes, not our slides |
| 5–15 | Wins this quarter | Top 3 wins, each with the number that proves it |
| 15–25 | Adoption + value realised | What's being used, what isn't, and what it returned |
| 25–32 | Risks + blockers | Honest read — what could go wrong, and the plan |
| 32–40 | Expansion / what's next | Where they could get more value, tied to their goals |
| 40–45 | Next steps | Owners and dates, agreed in the room |

## Notes to capture

- **Wins:** what happened, the number, who cares.
- **Adoption:** products/features in use vs. available; what's stuck and why.
- **Value realised:** time saved, revenue influenced, cost avoided — only numbers you can stand behind.
- **Risks:** stakeholder changes, usage drops, unresolved tickets, budget pressure.
- **Expansion:** candidate, business case, who needs to say yes.
- **Next steps:** action, owner, date. No ownerless actions.

## After the meeting

- Send the follow-up email the same day (use the meeting follow-up prompt).
- Log the risks in your health review. Update the account score if anything changed.
