# Onboarding Checklist — 30/60/90 Days

Use this with the onboarding plan prompt in `ai-prompts-for-cs.md`. Tick items off as they're done, and record the date — a checklist with no dates is just a wish list.

## Days 1–30: Get live

- [ ] Kick-off call held — goals, team, and timeline agreed in the customer's words
- [ ] Main contacts confirmed (day-to-day user + decision maker)
- [ ] Access and accounts set up; first login confirmed
- [ ] Success criteria written down and shared with the customer
- [ ] First quick win delivered (one small, visible result)
- [ ] Support channel explained — where to ask, what response time to expect
- [ ] Week 2 check-in done; blockers logged

**Done when:** The customer is live and has seen one real result.

## Days 31–60: Build the habit

- [ ] Core workflow in regular use (not a one-off test)
- [ ] Training session held for the wider team
- [ ] Adoption checked against the Day 30 baseline — up, flat, or down?
- [ ] First piece of feedback collected and acted on
- [ ] Champion identified — someone inside who will push usage when you're not there
- [ ] Risks logged: who hasn't adopted, and why

**Done when:** Usage is a habit for the core team, not a favour to you.

## Days 61–90: Prove the value

- [ ] Value review against the Day 1 success criteria — met, partly met, or missed?
- [ ] Results shared with the decision maker, with numbers
- [ ] Expansion or next-phase conversation started (if the value is there)
- [ ] Handover to steady-state rhythm agreed — cadence, owner, next QBR date
- [ ] Retrospective: what worked, what to change for the next onboarding

**Done when:** The customer can say, in their own words, what they got out of it.
